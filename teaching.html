Teaching, Supervision, and Service

I teach courses in algebra, cryptography, and the mathematical foundations of computer science. Teaching material for my courses is made available through AAU Moodle.

At Aalborg University, teaching is embedded in the university’s problem- and project-based learning (PBL) model, in which coursework is complemented by project work and supervision.

Teaching

  • Algebra and linear algebra: Algebra 1, Algebra 2, Applications of Algebraic Structures, and Linear Algebra and its Applications 2, mainly at Aalborg University between 2022 and 2026.
  • Coding theory and cryptography: Cryptography at Aalborg University, Ph.D.-level teaching on error-correcting codes and cryptographic applications, and advanced courses and lectures on minimal codes, post-quantum cryptography, cloud encryption, and elliptic curves.
  • Mathematical foundations and related teaching: Mathematical Foundations of Computer Science at Aalborg University, together with earlier teaching assistance in geometry and coding theory courses in Trento and at Politecnico di Milano.

Service

  • Member of the Censors for Civil Engineering programs in Denmark.
  • Member of the department council, Department of Mathematical Sciences, Aalborg University.
  • Organizer of special sessions in algebraic coding theory and cryptography.
  • Member of local organizing committees, including WCC 2024.
  • Reviewer for international journals including IEEE Transactions on Information Theory, Finite Fields and their Applications, Designs, Codes, and Cryptography, and others.
