Teaching, and Service

I teach courses in algebra, cryptography, and the mathematical foundations of computer science. Teaching material for my courses is made available through AAU Moodle.

At Aalborg University, teaching is embedded in the university’s problem- and project-based learning (PBL) model, in which coursework is complemented by project work and supervision.
