Teaching, supervision and service

Teaching

I teach and supervise projects in algebra, coding theory, cryptography, mathematical foundations of computer science, discrete mathematics, and related areas.

Recent courses and activities

  • Mathematical foundations of computer science, Aalborg University.
  • Algebra 1 and Algebra 2, Aalborg University.
  • Cryptography, Aalborg University.
  • Applications of algebraic structures, Aalborg University.
  • Ph.D. course module on error-correcting codes and cryptographic applications.

Supervision

I supervise and co-supervise students and postdoctoral researchers on topics in coding theory, cryptography, algebraic geometry codes, rank-metric codes, lightweight cryptography, and related areas.

Service

  • Member of the department council, Department of Mathematical Sciences, Aalborg University.
  • Organizer of special sessions in algebraic coding theory and cryptography.
  • Reviewer for international journals and conferences in coding theory, algebra, cryptography, and discrete mathematics.
  • Member of UMI and De Componendis Cifris.
