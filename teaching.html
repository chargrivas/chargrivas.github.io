Teaching, Supervision, and Service

I teach courses in algebra, cryptography, and the mathematical foundations of computer science. Teaching material for my courses is made available through AAU Moodle.

At Aalborg University, teaching is embedded in the university’s problem- and project-based learning (PBL) model, in which coursework is complemented by project work and supervision.

Teaching

  • Algebra and linear algebra: Algebra 1, Algebra 2, Applications of Algebraic Structures, and Linear Algebra and its Applications, between 2022 and 2026.
  • Coding theory and cryptography: Cryptography, Ph.D.-level teaching on error-correcting codes and cryptographic applications, and advanced courses and lectures on minimal codes, post-quantum cryptography, cloud encryption, and elliptic curves.
  • Mathematical foundations and related teaching: Mathematical Foundations of Computer Science, together with earlier teaching assistance in geometry and coding theory courses in Trento and at Politecnico di Milano.
