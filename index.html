Charisios Grivas

Assistant Professor - Department of Mathematical Sciences - Aalborg University

I am an Assistant Professor of Mathematical Economics at the Department of Mathematical Sciences, Aalborg University, and a data scientist working at the intersection of econometrics and risk modeling.

My research spans financial econometrics and climate econometrics, centering on resampling methods for hypothesis testing, high-dimensional model selection, and nonparametric methods, with applications to financial and macroeconomic data. I am particularly interested in developing statistically rigorous, computationally practical tools that hold up under real-world data conditions rather than idealized assumptions.

My Research

My research centers on resampling methods for hypothesis testing, high-dimensional model selection, and data-driven tuning of nonparametric methods, with applications to financial and macroeconomic data.

I also apply state-of-the-art econometric and machine learning techniques to the analysis of financial and macro-financial risk, as well as climate econometrics.
