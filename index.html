Matteo Bonini

Associate Professor — Aalborg University

I am an Associate Professor at the Department of Mathematical Sciences, Aalborg University, and Orient’s Fond Chair Associate Professor.

My research focuses on coding theory, algebra, geometry and cryptography, with emphasis on algebraic and geometric methods for coding theory and applications.
