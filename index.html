About me

I work on algebraic and geometric methods in coding theory, with applications to rank-metric codes, sum-rank metric codes, algebraic- geometry codes, minimal codes, covering codes, and cryptographic constructions.

Before joining Aalborg University, I was an IRC Postdoctoral Fellow at University College Dublin and a postdoctoral fellow at the University of Trento. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of Trento.
