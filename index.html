About Me

I was born in Perugia, Italy, and I am currently based at Aalborg University in Denmark. Before joining Aalborg University, I was an IRC Postdoctoral Fellow at University College Dublin and a postdoctoral fellow at the University of Trento. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of Trento, awarded cum laude.

Research

I work on algebraic and geometric methods in coding theory, with applications to cryptography and distributed systems. My interests include rank-metric codes, sum-rank metric codes, algebraic-geometric codes, minimal codes, covering codes, finite fields, and related combinatorial and geometric structures.
