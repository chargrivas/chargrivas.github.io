Matteo Bonini

Associate Professor — Aalborg University

I am an Associate Professor at the Department of Mathematical Sciences, Aalborg University, and Orient’s Fond Chair Associate Professor. My research lies in coding theory, algebra, geometry and cryptography, with particular focus on algebraic and geometric methods in coding theory.

AAU profile | ORCID | LinkedIn | arXiv

Research interests

  • Coding theory
  • Algebraic geometry codes
  • Rank-metric and sum-rank codes
  • Minimal and covering codes
  • Cryptographic applications
