Matteo Bonini

Associate Professor — Aalborg University

I am an Associate Professor at the Department of Mathematical Sciences at Aalborg University and Orient’s Fond Chair Associate Professor. My research focuses on coding theory, algebra, geometry, and cryptography, with emphasis on algebraic and geometric methods for coding theory and applications.

Research

My research lies in algebraic coding theory and its interactions with algebraic geometry, finite fields, and cryptography. I am particularly interested in:

  • Rank-metric and sum-rank codes
  • Algebraic-geometric codes
  • Covering and minimal codes
  • Applications to cryptography and distributed systems
